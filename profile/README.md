## Hi — I'm the person behind Heuralux

I'm an engineer who likes building useful things. My day job is building
high-performance scientific software and custom AI/ML systems. Heuralux is my
side of the bench — a small shop where I do that same kind of work at a
friendlier scale, for the local businesses and everyday folks who could use a
sharp tool but don't have a software team on staff.

That's really all this is: take a real problem, build something that genuinely
helps. No buzzwords, no sales team, no trying to sell you "AI" you don't need.

<br>

## What I can build for you

What I'm best at is sitting down with someone who knows their work inside and
out, and building a tool that fits the way they *actually* do it. You're the
expert on your business; I'm the expert on turning that into working software.
We figure out the right-sized tool together — no more, no less than the problem
calls for.

That might look like:

- **A model** that forecasts your demand, pricing, or inventory from the data
  you already have.
- **An assistant** that takes the tedious, repetitive part of your week off
  your plate.
- **A small app or script** that turns an afternoon of manual work into the
  press of a button.
- **An honest answer** about whether AI can help you at all — sometimes it
  can't, and I'll say so.

I'm starting small and on purpose: I take on a handful of custom projects at a
time. And if you just want to talk something through — to find out whether it's
even possible, or where to start — I'm always happy to do that, no strings
attached.

<br>

## A little about me

<div align="center">
<img src="./assets/heuralux-compass.png" alt="Horizon Compass mark" width="120">
</div>

<br>

I've spent about 15 years building simulation, modeling, and machine-learning
software — the kind of work that usually lives inside research labs and
engineering teams. I have a PhD in engineering, and most of my career has gone
into translating messy, real-world problems into models and tools that people
can actually use.

Heuralux is my way of bringing that same skill set down to earth — to the
smaller businesses and individuals who rarely get access to it. I'm not trying
to build an empire. I just like good problems and building things that work.

The name says the rest: **HEURISKO** — *to discover* — and **LUX** — *light*.
There's a lot of hype and noise around AI right now. My goal is simply to cut
through it and build a few things that genuinely help.
